You can now choose what Kello's popover shows. The new Popover page in Settings lists its sections: drag them into the order you like and switch off the ones you don't need. The month grid, the toolbar and the list always stay; today's counts, the world clocks and the Next up card are yours to arrange.

Reordering the date in the menu bar works the same way now, with a line that shows where a row will land and Move Up and Move Down for the keyboard and VoiceOver.
